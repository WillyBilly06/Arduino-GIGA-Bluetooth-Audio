## Oracle and feature limits

- SBC/SBC-XQ,AAC-LC ADTS/LATM,aptX,aptX HD and Opus used pinned PCM16 comparisons at the listed configurations. An aptX HD PCM16 comparison does not establish independent 24-bit fidelity.

- HE-AAC v1 used existing local SBR-only 44.1 kHz mono/stereo references. Those media are excluded from distribution. PS/HEv2,xHE and other rates/configurations are not qualified.
- Opus 48 kHz stereo 20 ms fixture execution is separate from phone negotiation, sender interoperability or other frame durations.

- Total phone-to-DAC 200 ms latency, low-bit fidelity, physical I2S/acoustics and longer continuous operation remain unqualified. HFP/HSP/RFCOMM/SCO/microphone and complete typed AVRCP remain unfinished.
